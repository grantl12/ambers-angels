Budget Justification (The "Why")
Infrastructure Efficiency: Unlike traditional law enforcement tech that costs $50k+ per unit, Amber’s Angels leverages a "Bring Your Own Drone" (BYOD) model. This budget covers the cloud-based "brain" of the system rather than expensive hardware.

In-Kind Matching: We estimate that for every $1,000 in grant funding, our volunteer pilots provide over $5,000 in flight hours and search capability. This "5x Impact" is a major selling point for local Georgia donors.

Scalability: This 6-month pilot serves as a "proof of concept" that can be duplicated across other Georgia counties using the same cloud infrastructure.
