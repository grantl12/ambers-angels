How It Works

A search network that activates in minutes

01
🔔

Alert Fires

FEMA IPAWS issues an AMBER Alert. Our platform detects it within 60 seconds and pushes a mission notification to enrolled volunteers in the area.

02
🚗

Volunteers Deploy

Volunteers accept the mission in the app and drive their assigned sector — or simply their normal route — with the app scanning their camera feed in real time.

03
🤖

AI Identifies

Our Cascade Inference engine checks every frame for the suspect vehicle — plate, make, model, color, and year range. Frames are deleted immediately after processing.

04
👤

Human Reviews

Every AI detection is reviewed by a trained coordinator before anything reaches law enforcement. No automated alerts — ever.

05
🚔

Law Enforcement Acts

Confirmed leads are sent to law enforcement with GPS coordinates, timestamp, and vehicle details — a complete lead package, not just a plate number.

Alert Types

We don't only search for children

Our platform activates for any government-issued missing person emergency — because every vulnerable life deserves the same urgency.

AMBER Alert

Missing Children

Activated when a child under 18 is believed to have been abducted. Highest priority. Time-critical.

Silver Alert

Missing Seniors

Issued for adults with dementia or Alzheimer's who have wandered or gone missing.

Mattie's Call

Adults with Disabilities

Georgia's alert for at-risk adults with developmental or intellectual disabilities.

Purple Alert

Persons with Disabilities

For missing individuals with a broad range of disabilities who may need immediate assistance.

Blue Alert

Endangered Officers

Issued when a law enforcement officer is missing or endangered. Volunteers exercise extra caution.

Privacy by Design

Technology that saves lives — not a surveillance tool

Every architectural decision we've made prevents the platform from becoming something it should never be. These aren't policies we can override — they're built into the system.

🗑️

No Video Storage

Frames are analyzed in memory and deleted immediately. We never write raw footage to disk.

⚡

Event-Triggered Only

Detection is dormant between alerts. There is no passive background scanning — ever.

✋

Human-in-the-Loop

No AI result reaches law enforcement without a trained human coordinator reviewing it first.

🔒

Minimum Data

We only collect what the active mission requires. GPS telemetry is deleted after 90 days.

🙈

Faces Obfuscated

Our pipeline automatically obfuscates faces and non-suspect plates of innocent bystanders.
