About the Founder

Grant Lindberg
Executive Director

Founder & Executive Director — Amber’s Angels
US Army Veteran Sergeant First Class (E-7) NSA • Fourth Amendment Auditor SDVOSB Founder

From air defense to child safety

Grant served 11.5 years in the U.S. Army, making Sergeant First Class (E-7) in nine years. He started as an air defense artillery specialist, moved to US Army Space Command in 2010, and attended the Defense Language Institute in 2013 for Korean. His final posting was NSA Korea, where one of his roles was as a Fourth Amendment auditor — reviewing signals collection activities for legal and constitutional compliance.

“The privacy-first architecture in this platform isn’t a marketing decision. It’s what someone builds when they’ve spent years on the other side of that question.”

After separating in 2015, Grant joined MasterCard as a DB2 database administrator, then led the scrum organization for their GDPR compliance implementation. He contracted with Leidos on federal programs, and has been at Oversight Systems in Atlanta since 2020 — leading the federal customer team and managing the engagement with the GSA SmartPay 3 program, the largest commercial charge card program in the federal government.

The concept for Amber’s Angels formed in 2016, when Grant acquired his first DJI drone and recognized that the live video feed could be directed to any analysis pipeline. At the same time, he was tracking commercial advances in LPR and OCR technology. He connected the two ideas and built toward a platform until the technology was ready to execute the vision properly.

Grant lives in Carrollton, Georgia with his wife Kessie — also a U.S. Army veteran and Korean linguist — and their five daughters. That last part isn’t incidental.
