What happens next
1
Review (within 24 h) Our team verifies your profile and activates your account. You'll get a push notification the moment you're approved.
2
Download the app Install Amber's Angels on your phone. Sign in with the account you just created — the app will show a pending badge until approval clears.
3
Join your first mission When an alert fires in your area you'll be notified instantly. Open the app, start a session, and the system does the rest.
🚗 Phone-in-car volunteers — quick safety note Mount your phone on the windshield or dashboard using a secure holder before you start driving. Never handle the phone while in motion — the app runs automatically once a session is started. Pull over safely before adjusting any settings.
