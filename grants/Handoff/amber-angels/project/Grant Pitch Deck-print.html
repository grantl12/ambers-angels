The Solution

Turn smartphones into a distributed search grid.

AMBER Angels is a privacy-first volunteer network that activates during government-issued alerts. Approved community members run our app while driving normal routes — providing law enforcement with real-time, AI-screened, human-reviewed leads.

01

Zero hardware barrier

Volunteers use the smartphones they already own. A car mount is the only physical equipment. Onboarding is hours, not weeks.

→ Scales to volunteer headcount, not hardware budget
02

Cascade Inference Engine

YOLOv8 + MobileNetV3 + OpenALPR identify make, model, year range, and plate. Vehicles are findable even when plates are switched.

→ <5% false positive rate vs ~30% plate-only
03

Privacy-first by design

Raw footage deleted immediately. No video archive. No surveillance database. Every detection requires human coordinator review.

→ Community-trusted, not surveillance infrastructure

Privacy First

Saving lives without building a surveillance state.

Privacy is the foundation, not a feature. Every architectural choice begins with: what is the minimum data required for this mission? It's why we can operate where commercial vendors face community pushback.

No video archive

Footage is processed in real-time and deleted immediately after AI scoring. No searchable database.

Operational necessity only

Strictly mission-required data is collected. Nothing more. Retention measured in seconds, not days.

Human in the loop

No AI result reaches law enforcement without coordinator review. AI flags. Humans decide.

Public transparency

Quarterly published reports on accuracy, retention, and incident counts. Auditable by design.
