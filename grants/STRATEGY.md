# Amber’s Angels: Grant Strategy & Mission

## 1. Updated Grant Executive Summary (The "Elevator Pitch")
**The Amber's Angels Advantage: Mobilized Community ALPR**

While traditional ALPR (Automatic License Plate Recognition) is limited by fixed locations and high infrastructure costs, Amber's Angels provides a decentralized, volunteer-driven network. Our core differentiator is the trusted coordination layer that allows citizens to safely volunteer their mobile sensor data specifically during active AMBER Alerts.

### The Solution:
*   **On-Demand Infrastructure:** We transform existing volunteer devices into a mobile ALPR network the moment a FEMA IPAWS alert is triggered.
*   **Closing the Search Gap:** Our network covers residential streets and rural routes that stationary cameras cannot reach.
*   **VMMC Verification Layer:** We use Vehicle Make, Model, and Color (VMMC) as a secondary verification engine to provide law enforcement with high-confidence, actionable leads.
