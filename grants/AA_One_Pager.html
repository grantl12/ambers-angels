The Problem
76% of abducted children who are murdered are killed within the first 3 hours. Law enforcement responds fast — but fixed infrastructure has limits. When a suspect vehicle turns off a covered road, Flock goes dark. Plates get covered. Plates get swapped. Every residential street, rural route, and secondary road without a fixed camera is a gap. In an abduction, that gap can be fatal.
Our Solution
When a FEMA IPAWS alert fires, Amber's Angels activates a network of community volunteers — phones mounted in cars and FAA-licensed drones in the air — covering the roads between your fixed cameras in real time. Our AI identifies vehicles by plate and by make, model, color, and body type — so even when a plate is obscured or swapped, we can still surface a lead. Every detection is reviewed by a human coordinator before it reaches law enforcement.
Join Swarm — Remote Drone Dispatch

A volunteer powers on their drone from home, taps Join Swarm, and the mission coordinator takes remote control — directing the drone to coverage gaps on the map without the volunteer ever leaving their driveway.

BVLOS waiver holders: full coordinator control, extended range. Standard Part 107: geofenced to volunteer location automatically. FAA certification is enforced in software — not the honor system.

How It Works
1
FEMA issues alert — suspect vehicle profile loaded automatically into our watchlist
2
Volunteers notified — phone volunteers and drone pilots deploy to assigned sectors
3
AI analyzes frames — plate recognition + vehicle make / model / color classification
4
Coordinator reviews every hit — plate confidence, vehicle match, GPS, frame image
5
Confirmed lead delivered — GPS coordinates, plate, vehicle description, timestamp
