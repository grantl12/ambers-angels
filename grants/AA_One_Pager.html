The Problem
76% of abducted children who are murdered are killed within the first 3 hours. Law enforcement responds fast, but fixed camera infrastructure has limits. When a suspect vehicle turns off a covered road, the trail goes cold. Plates get covered. Plates get swapped. Every residential street, rural highway, and parking lot without a camera is a gap. In an abduction, that gap can be fatal.
Our Solution
When a government alert fires, Amber's Angels activates a volunteer network — phones mounted in cars scanning plates automatically, and FAA-licensed drones deployed to coverage gaps. Our AI identifies vehicles by plate and by make, model, color, and body type, so even when a plate is obscured or swapped, we can still surface a lead. Every detection is reviewed by a human coordinator before any information reaches law enforcement.

How It Works
1
Government alert fires — FEMA, NWS, NCMEC, NamUs, or law enforcement BOLO. Vehicle profile loaded into watchlist automatically.
2
Volunteers activate — push notification to phones and drone pilots in the alert area. Phone volunteers scan passively from their car — no driving changes needed.
3
AI analyzes in real time — on-device plate recognition (phones) + server-side ALPR and YOLOv8 vehicle classification (drones). Phone scanning runs entirely on-device — no images leave volunteer phones. Drone feeds are processed server-side with strict retention controls.
4
Coordinator reviews every match — confidence score, vehicle description, GPS, evidence frame. No automated reports to law enforcement.
5
Confirmed lead delivered — GPS coordinates, plate, vehicle description, timestamp, and evidence frame to responding agency.
Autonomous Drone Dispatch — Built and Tested

A volunteer powers on their drone at home, taps Join Swarm, and the mission coordinator directs the aircraft to coverage gaps on the live map. The drone flies a programmed waypoint mission autonomously — the volunteer never leaves their driveway. Lawnmower sweep patterns cover parking lots at 15m altitude. If a medium-confidence match appears mid-flight, the drone pauses automatically for sharper imaging, then resumes. FAA Part 107 certification enforced in software — BVLOS requires admin-verified waiver on file.
