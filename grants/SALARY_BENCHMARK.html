Executive Compensation Benchmark

Prepared in support of the Founder Services Agreement  ·  Amber's Angels, Inc.  ·  May 2026
Purpose: document IRS "reasonable compensation" basis for Grant Lindberg as CEO/CTO

IRS regulations require that compensation paid to officers and key employees of 501(c)(3) organizations be "reasonable" — defined as the amount that would ordinarily be paid for like services by like enterprises under like circumstances. The following benchmark establishes that a salary of $120,000–$180,000 per year is reasonable for the role of Founder/CEO/CTO at Amber's Angels.

Role Description

Grant Lindberg serves as the founding executive of Amber's Angels, performing the combined functions of Chief Executive Officer and Chief Technology Officer for an early-stage nonprofit technology organization. Specific responsibilities include:

  • Full-stack software architecture and development (FastAPI, Next.js, PostgreSQL, React Native)
  • AI/ML pipeline design and integration (YOLOv8, OpenALPR, Plate Recognizer)
  • Cloud infrastructure and DevOps (DigitalOcean, PM2, GitHub Actions, nginx)
  • Mobile application development (Expo React Native, Android Kotlin native modules, Apple App Store)
  • Executive leadership: strategy, fundraising, government relations, grant writing support
  • Pilot program management: Carroll County, GA deployment coordination
  • Regulatory compliance: FAA Part 107/108, FEMA IPAWS, IRS 501(c)(3)

IRS Rebuttable Presumption of Reasonableness

Under IRC § 4958 and Treasury Regulation § 53.4958-6, a nonprofit board can establish a "rebuttable presumption" that compensation is reasonable by following a three-step process:

  1. Approval by an independent body. Compensation must be approved by the board of directors (or a compensation committee) composed of individuals who do not have a conflict of interest. Action required: board vote, recorded in minutes.
  2. Reliance on comparable data. The approving body must obtain and rely upon appropriate comparability data prior to making its determination. This document satisfies that requirement.
  3. Documentation. The basis for the determination must be documented in writing contemporaneously (within 60 days of the decision). Action required: board minutes reference this benchmark by name and date.

If these three steps are followed, the IRS bears the burden of proving the compensation is unreasonable. Without them, the burden shifts to the organization.

Conclusion

Based on the comparable data above, the Board of Directors of Amber's Angels, Inc. finds that annual compensation of $120,000–$180,000, plus standard benefits (health insurance, retirement contribution up to IRS limits, expense reimbursement), is reasonable for Grant Lindberg in his capacity as founding CEO/CTO. A specific figure within this range shall be set by the board at the time of formal employment offer, taking into account the Organization's funding level and cash flow at that time.
