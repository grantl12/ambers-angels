**RE: Funding Inquiry — Carroll County Rapid Recovery Pilot Program**

Dear [Grant Officer Name],

On behalf of Amber's Angels Inc., a Georgia-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit in formation and a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned entity, I am writing to inquire about potential support for our **Carroll County Rapid Recovery Pilot Program**.

**The Problem**

Every year, the "Golden Hour" following a child abduction or missing person report determines whether that person comes home safely. While law enforcement does an exceptional job, they are limited by stationary technology and resource gaps — particularly on residential streets and rural routes that fixed license plate readers cannot cover.

**Our Innovation**

Amber's Angels bridges this gap by deploying a distributed, volunteer-driven network of smartphone and drone sensor nodes that activate automatically when a FEMA IPAWS AMBER Alert is issued. Unlike traditional systems, our platform uses custom AI (YOLOv8 + MobileNetV3) to identify suspect vehicles based on **Vehicle Make, Model, and Color (VMMC)** — providing actionable leads even when license plates are obscured. Our **"Privacy-by-Design"** architecture ensures that no raw video is ever archived, and no surveillance database is built.

**The Request**

We are seeking a one-time grant of **$5,000** to fund a 6-month pilot program in Carroll County, Georgia. This funding will cover:

- Cloud-based AI inference infrastructure (DigitalOcean)
- FAA Part 107 Pilot Training scholarships for 3 local volunteer drone pilots
- Field sensing hardware for mobile search nodes (dedicated devices + mounts)
- Liability insurance and data security compliance audit

**Why Now**

We have secured a Letter of Support from local law enforcement, completed our EIN registration (IRS CP 575 on file), and assembled a core volunteer team. Grant funding is the final step to operational readiness.

As a veteran-led organization, we believe in technical excellence and community accountability. We would welcome the opportunity to submit a full proposal or provide a live technical demonstration of our VMMC detection capabilities.

Thank you for your time and for your commitment to Georgia's safety.
